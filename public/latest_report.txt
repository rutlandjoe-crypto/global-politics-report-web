HEADLINE
Judge tosses Trump's attempt to block Alaska Senate results if GOP incumbent loses

SNAPSHOT
A federal judge has dismissed a highly unusual lawsuit by the Trump administration that tried to block the certification of results from Alaska's U.S. Senate race if the Republican incumbent lost.
